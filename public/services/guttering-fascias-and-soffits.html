Guttering, fascias and soffits

Nobody rings a roofer about a gutter until the wall inside is damp. By then the gutter has usually been overflowing for two winters.

What a failing gutter costs you

Water running down a wall instead of into a downpipe soaks the brickwork, blows render, and wets the timber ends of the roof where they sit on the wall plate. Inside, it shows up as a damp patch high on a wall or a stain at the top of a chimney breast, and it gets blamed on rising damp more often than it deserves.

The repair while it is still a gutter is small. The repair once the fascia and the rafter ends have gone is a different job entirely, which is the whole argument for clearing them.

What we actually do

Clear and flush the run, then check the falls with water rather than by eye, because a gutter that looks level often is not. Realign brackets, replace failed unions and stop ends, and renew sections where the plastic has gone brittle. Cast iron gets repaired where it is worth keeping, which on a period property it usually is.

Where the fascia behind has rotted, replacing the gutter alone is pointless: there is nothing sound to fix it to. We replace fascia and soffit in uPVC or aluminium, and we check the felt and the eaves ventilation while the edge is open, because that is the moment to fix it.

What gets confused with it

A blocked gulley at ground level backs up and soaks a wall in exactly the same place as an overflowing gutter, and has nothing to do with the roof. So does a downpipe that discharges onto a path instead of into a drain. We check both before quoting anything at high level.

Maintenance, which is cheaper than any of this

Annual clearing, in autumn after the leaves are down, is the single most cost-effective thing anyone does to a roof. Nearly every escalated leak we attend on a commercial building started in a blocked gutter or a blocked valley outlet.

Common questions

Do you clear gutters as a standalone job?+

Yes, and we will send you photographs of what came out and of anything we noticed while we were up there.

Cast iron or plastic?+

Cast iron on a period house where it suits the building, and we repair rather than replace where the run is sound. Plastic or aluminium elsewhere.
